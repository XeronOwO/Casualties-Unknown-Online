# Glossary

English | [中文](glossary.zh.md)

The words this mod uses, explained in everyday language. A term appears here the first time a guide
page needs it; the technical definition of the same word lives in the reference glossary.

## Session words

- **Host** — the player whose game owns the world.
- **Guest** — a player who joined the host's world.
- **Lobby** — the Steam room that carries the group before and during a session.
- **Session** — one host together with its connected guests.
- **World** — the saved place you play in; it survives between sessions.
- **Layer** — one level of the world; a run moves through layers.
- **Run** — one playthrough, from entering the world to dying or finishing it.

## Action words

- **Carry** — one player physically carrying another.
- **Unconscious** — alive but unable to act, waiting for help.
- **Revive** — bringing an unconscious or dead teammate back at a trader.
- **Respawn** — the game putting a dead player back into the world.
- **Permadeath** — the setting where death is final, with no revive or respawn afterwards.

## Technical words you may see

- **Kernel** — the part of the mod that owns the authoritative state.
- **Command** — a request for something to happen; it may be refused.
- **Event** — a fact the game has accepted, which everyone will follow.
- **Checkpoint** — a complete copy of the world state, used when someone joins.
- **Projection** — a view built from the authoritative state, such as the objects you see.
- **Protocol version** — the number both sides compare when a guest joins.
