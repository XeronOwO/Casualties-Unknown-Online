# CUO term registry — the only source of Chinese renderings.
# Format: English | 中文 | first use on a Chinese page | never render as | notes
# A Chinese page writes the 中文 column; the first time a term appears it uses the
# “first use” spelling and 中文 alone afterwards. A new term is added here before it is
# used, so two pages cannot invent two renderings of one mechanism.
# Readers meet these words through docs/zh/reference/glossary.md (English: docs/en/reference/glossary.md).
#

command | 指令 | 指令（command） | 命令 | a typed request to the kernel

event | 事件 | 事件 | 消息 | a fact the kernel accepted
batch | 批次 | 批次（batch） | 数据包，包 | one atomic committed set of facts

epoch | 运行纪元 | 运行纪元（epoch） | 时代，周期 | run identity; stale traffic from an old run is rejected
native | 原生 | 原生（native） | 本地，官方 | the game's own code and behaviour
patch | 补丁 | 补丁（patch） | 插件 | one Harmony hook; a plugin is 插件
mod | 模组 | 模组（mod） | 模块， MOD | third-party content loaded through the mod API
adapter | 适配器 | 适配器（Game Adapter） | 转接头，适配层 | the only layer that knows the game's private types
runtime | 运行时 | 运行时（Runtime） | 运行环境 | the stable CUO layer: protocol, session, mod loading
snapshot | 快照 | 快照（snapshot） | 截图 | a point-in-time copy of state
carry | 背负 | 背负（carry） | 携带 | one player carrying another; carried items are 随身物品
revive | 救活 | 救活（revive） | 复活 | bringing a downed teammate back at a trader
respawn | 重生 | 重生（respawn） | 复活 | the game placing a dead player back into the world
permadeath | 永久死亡 | 永久死亡（permadeath） | 一命模式 | death is terminal
item | 物品 | 物品 | 道具 | anything the kernel tracks as an item
container | 容器 | 容器 | 箱子 | an item that holds other items
capability | 能力 | 能力（capability） | 功能 | per-type composed behaviour such as battery or liquid
protocol version | 协议版本 | 协议版本 | 游戏版本 | checked when a guest joins
handshake | 握手 | 握手（handshake） | 连接协商 | the join-time version and identity exchange
lease | 租约 | 租约（lease） | 锁 | `world.lease` names the process writing a world folder
cut | 切片 | 切片（cut） | 裁剪，分段 | one save write of the live world
backup | 备份 | 备份 | 副本 | a compressed copy of an earlier cut
world archive | 世界归档 | 世界归档（world archive） | 存档文件，存档包 | the CUO save layout: one folder per world
remote clone | 远端克隆体 | 远端克隆体（remote clone） | 替身，影子 | the local stand-in showing another player's character
nameplate | 名牌 | 名牌（nameplate） | 姓名牌 | the label above a character
off-screen arrow | 屏幕外箭头 | 屏幕外箭头（off-screen arrow） | 边缘指示器 | points at a teammate outside the view
pinyin search | 拼音搜索 | 拼音搜索（pinyin search） | 拼音输入 | lets native search boxes match Chinese by pinyin
world generation | 世界生成 | 世界生成（world generation） | 地图生成 | deterministic generation from the run's seed and settings
host rules | 主机规则 | 主机规则（host rules） | 房主设置 | host-owned gameplay switches
interaction panel | 交互面板 | 交互面板（interaction panel） | 操作面板 | the CUO panel opened with the configured key, `F6` by default

unconscious | 昏迷 | 昏迷（unconscious） | 倒地 | alive but unable to act
deterministic | 确定性 | 确定性（deterministic） | 固定 | same inputs produce the same result
seed | 种子 | 种子（seed） | 随机数 | the world-generation input carried in the run baseline
gate | 门禁 | 门禁（gate） | 检查，关卡 | a repository check that refuses a change breaking a rule; the gates run as tests
baseline | 基线 | 基线（baseline） | 基准，底稿 | a recorded, reviewed state a gate compares the tree against
evidence | 证据 | 证据 | 证明，凭据 | the recorded proof for a claim: a file, a command result or a runtime trace
review | 复核 | 复核（review） | 评审，审查 | the independent check a change passes before delivery
process record | 流程记录 | 流程记录 | 历史记录，过程文档 | a document recording what a cycle did (backlog, evidence, decisions); never translated
breadcrumb | 面包屑 | 面包屑 | 导航条，路径栏 | the navigation line at the head and tail of a page naming its place
judgment ownership | 判定归属 | 判定归属（judgment ownership） | 决定权，归属判定 | which machine decides what happens to a player: that player's own client, on its own view and timeline
read model | 只读模型 | 只读模型（read model） | 只读数据 | projected state meant for reading; a reading is never an arbitration input or a verdict
vitals | 生命体征 | 生命体征（vitals） | 血量，生命值 | the projected physiological fields of one player: health, hunger, thirst, stamina, energy, temperature

namespace | 命名空间 | 命名空间（namespace） | 前缀，域 | the mod-declared first half of a content id; context separates it from the C# keyword
content kind | 内容类别 | 内容类别（content kind） | 内容类型 | the `kind` string a definition is registered under, such as `item` or `recipe`
schema version | 结构版本 | 结构版本（schema version） | 架构版本，格式版本号 | the mod-owned version stored with an opaque persisted payload; the framework never migrates it

rollback | 回滚 | 回滚（rollback） | 撤销，退回 | undoing a locally applied action after arbitration refuses the claim
tombstone | 墓碑 | 墓碑（tombstone） | 标记，记号 | a recorded refusal that stops a later repeat of the same creation
envelope | 信封 | 信封（envelope） | 包，封包 | one framed kernel message: a command, a committed batch, a checkpoint or a state stream
domain | 领域 | 领域 | 模块，子系统 | one slice of the kernel state with its own typed model and rules: items, players, enemies, fluids
effect | 效果 | 效果 | 影响，特效 | what an outer layer must do after a committed batch; derived by projections, never stored in the batch
reduce | 归约 | 归约 | 简化，应用 | applying the accepted events of a batch to the authoritative state, the same way on every machine
permission | 权限 | 权限 | 许可，授权 | a capability a mod declares on its attribute; CUO grants nothing implicitly
network mode | 网络模式 | 网络模式（network mode） | 联机模式，网络类型 | a mod's contract with the session: who must have it, and where it runs
lifecycle | 生命周期 | 生命周期 | 生存期，寿命 | the calls the framework makes on a mod: bind, initialize, start, update, stop, dispose
seam | 接缝 | 接缝 | 接口，边界，缝 | a named boundary where one side may be checked or replaced, such as a cut seam
port | 端口 | 端口 | 接口，插口 | a narrow interface a capability is reached through; the adapter is composed of capability ports

contract | 契约 | 契约 | 接口，协议 | a surface with a stability promise; every other surface is an implementation
implementation | 实现 | 实现 | 底层，内部代码 | a surface with no promise: a mod may patch it, and it may change in any commit
public surface | 公开接口面 | 公开接口面 | 公开接口，公有 API | every public type and member a caller can reach, recorded in the baseline

runtime spawn | 运行时生成 | 运行时生成 | 动态生成，临时生成 | an entity created during play rather than by world generation
