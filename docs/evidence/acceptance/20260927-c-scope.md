## The scenario gap this run found

The batch was planned from the session-class slugs of the three local triage reports: 45 candidates, all
still in `review/`. The run established the committed driver's boundary — its closed vocabulary covers the
Online UI's session surface (`ping`, `state`, the window and its pages and controls, create/join/start).

Every other session ticket needs a setup path the driver does not carry: a carry relation with a limp
rider, a forced severe-sleepiness state, a native right-click context menu, a Tab / accelerate /
middle-click press, an eating or placement sound comparison. Those are per-scenario recipes that reach the
game's own entry points through ad-hoc evaluator snippets; none exists in the tree today, committed or
recorded. Closing those tickets is therefore scenario-recipe work, not another driver verb — and each
recipe belongs with the ticket that needs it.

The 45 candidates stay in `review/` with their class unchanged; this batch claims none of their rows,
because the capability a row needs is not the capability the driver proves (`unproven` is not `pass`).
They group as the triage grouped them:

- carry/piggyback cluster: the rider placement, idle-sit, unconscious, carrier-sit, container and
  carried-inventory tickets;
- pose/desync cluster: severe sleepiness, remote pose/head, fall-injury mouth;
- hit/visibility cluster: the hit flash, local hit determination, the remote context-menu visibility;
- sound cluster: metal-scrap placement, eating, background ghost sounds, the suppressed-call families
  (their audible halves remain residuals for the user);
- world/time and player state: the two acceleration tickets, the dead-player suffix, the Tab backpack;
- sync-recovery: command-loss reconciliation, runtime-entity backfill, session-control convergence,
  guest break drops;
- Online UI and single-client surfaces: the worlds/backup surface, the command console, the location
  marker, the solo menu-exit trigger;
- and the tickets whose world must be prepared first: the save/reconnect families, the 128-cap damage
  tables, the two-operator medical tickets and the determinism pass.

The per-slug classification, with each ticket's decisive quote and evidence kind, is the union of the
three triage reports under the local artifact directory (`triage-part-*.md`); the candidate list itself is
`candidates.txt` there.
