## What "C# acceptance" means here

- A row is a class under `tests/CasualtiesUnknownOnline.AcceptanceSuite/Cases/` registered in `CaseRegistry.All`; it
  drives the game through the in-process channel and asserts on readings.
- A recipe is the in-process primitive a case calls — C# deployed into the acceptance assembly, never a script the case
  shells out to. Recipes stay as primitives; the acceptance code is the case.

## Scope — the valuable rows still missing (each gets a case)

1. **Crafting outcome** — materials are consumed and the produced item lands in the bag (today the panel row only opens
   the panel and names a recipe).
2. **Two-member save/continue** — host plus two members, continue, both characters and their items survive.
3. **Real drag** — a mouse-staged drag of an item onto a body or into a container, the path a player's hand takes.
4. **Downed teammate** — a body knocked out, revived by a teammate's own hand, alive afterwards.
5. **Mid-run late join** — a member joins a world that has been running, without a rejoin ritual.
6. **Sample-mod content** — a custom item/liquid seeded by the example mod registers and is usable.
7. **Steam transport** — blocked on a second game-owning account; a named residual with its reading, not a row.
