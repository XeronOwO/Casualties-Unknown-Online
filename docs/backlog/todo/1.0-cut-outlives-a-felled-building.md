# A continue refuses a healthy row for a building the layer no longer has

The save/continue row stages a trap blast and then cuts. In one fresh batch the restore refused one
`building-health` row and called the world incomplete; the row's own audit read every other fact applied and
zero refused.

## Reading (2026-11-18, `.acceptance/full-after-toggles5`)

- Row: `save-continue`, FAIL 15.1 s on artifact `0.1.0+dd089dbb6`, case cut at `2026-11-17T19:54:55.031Z`.
- The cut `saves/w-20261117-eb53/live.cuoz` carried four world-entity rows: `trap-consumption` at (16,507)
  and `trap-state` x2 at (16,507), plus `building-health` at **(23,500) health 1008.12408**.
- Host log: `Building-entity health checkpoint projection applied (0/1 entities).` then
  `[WorldEntityKernel] the restored world-entity facts are dropped without reaching the live world: the
  regenerated layer has no entity at this cut's position (1 consumption(s), 0 opened entit(ies), 1 health row(s)).`
  and `[SaveFacts] the live world did NOT take 1 of 2 restored world-entity row(s)`.
- The audit line: `1 applied / 1 refused world-entity row(s), 1 row(s) not taken, restore INCOMPLETE (reported
  at error level, not retried).`
- The consumption row was accepted (the consumed mine is satisfied by our 2026-11-17 rule); the health row is
  the only refusal.
- Focused re-run on the same build (`.acceptance/savejoin-refusal1`, `-Suite save-continue -Fresh`): **PASS**
  36.0 s, zero refused. So the refusal is world-dependent, not a constant.

## Why the two halves disagree

The cut is taken about a second after the blast. The kernel's `building-health` row is the health the blast
reported; the game then fells buildings whose support the explosion removed, on its own schedule. The cut-time
reconcile (`CutBuildingHealthReconcile`, 2026-11-17) already re-reads the live world so a building missing at
the cut is recorded destroyed — but the fell can land **after** the cut, which leaves a positive-health row for
an entity the next regeneration does not have. The restore then correctly refuses it, and the world is marked
incomplete.

The other candidate is placement drift beyond the lookup's 3 m (`BuildingEntityLookup`); the 2026-11-17
readings measured drift up to about 2 m, and this row was not measured.

## Options

- A — cut-time settle: the trap-blast row waits for the game's support-loss pass before cutting (a bounded
  wait on "no structural collapse pending", or cutting at the next layer-stable frame). Consequence: the row
  costs a frame or two and the race is gone, but the cut gets a new timing rule to document.
- B — restore-side rule: a positive-health row whose position has no building in the regenerated layer is
  recorded as destroyed when the kernel's run also reports the entity gone. Consequence: a genuinely lost
  damaged building would stop being a red, which is exactly the signal we want; needs the kernel's entity
  lifetime, not just the position.
- C — measure first: run the focused row N times on fresh worlds, record the refusal rate and the distance
  between the recorded cell and the nearest live building in the cases that refuse. Consequence: no fix yet,
  but the next step is chosen by a number.

Recommendation: C, then A if the refusals cluster on rows cut within a second of a collapse; B only if a
refused row turns out to have its building standing a few metres away.
