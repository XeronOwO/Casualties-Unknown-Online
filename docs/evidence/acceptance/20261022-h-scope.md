## The fixtures (declared setup substitutions)

The receiver (guest) carries a `plasticbag` (its own rule refuses a 4 g item: the container-refusal row), a
non-container item in slot 0 (the substitution row), and is filled to every slot for the no-free-slot row. The
giver (host) carries the items the rows hand over: a `toolbox` (4 g, refused by the bag), an `emergencylight`

`item-provide mode=create` on the client that will carry them; the bodies are brought together with `body-place`.
