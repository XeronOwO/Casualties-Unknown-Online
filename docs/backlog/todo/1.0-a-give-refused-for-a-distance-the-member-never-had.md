# A give is refused for a distance the member never had

- Status: Todo
- Priority: High

## Reading

`give-item` failed in `.acceptance/twenty-3` with the member's bag empty: the host answered
`{"ok":true,"mode":"give-world","item":"bandage"}` and the member's own log refused the hand-over:

```
[PickupCheck] bandage refused — distance 93.1, line-of-sight blocked True.
[PickUpResult] bandage → world (slot 0) at (21.5,395.5).
```

The row had just placed the member beside the host (`member placed: {"ok":true,"fromX":7.94,"fromY":487.62,
"toX":7.96,"toY":491.42,"moved":true}`) and its own distance probe reads both ends under four units before the give.

## The line

The refusal is the game's own rule, replayed on the member's client: `Body.DoPickupCheck` (recorded by
`DoPickupCheckPatch`, `src/CasualtiesUnknownOnline.GameAdapter/Patches/…`), which measures the item against the
local body and the local line of sight.

## The chain

The host gives the item to the member's body; the member's client replays `PickUpToSlot`; the replay puts the item
at the world position carried in the instruction, and the local body stands at `(21.5,395.5)` — ninety-three units
from it — so the native check refuses and `PlayerInventoryTakeService.DeliverCreatedItem` falls through to the
world drop.

## Why this is the cause and not a symptom

The row's probe reads both ends under four units because it reads the host's record of the member, and that record
still holds the placed position while the member's own body is elsewhere. The item's authority position belongs to
the host's world; the member's body lives in its own. The give fails on the gap between them, not on the row's
placement.

## What to read next

1. Who writes the member's body position on the host side after a `body-place` — the placement is reported, and the
   host's clone is at the placed spot; the member's own body must be as well (read the member's `body-read` at the
   same instant as the host's `remote-body` record).
2. Whether the member's body was moved by the world (a fall) between the placement and the give, and whether that
   move is reported: the member's `(21.5,395.5)` is ninety units below and beside the placed spot.
3. Whether `PickUpToSlot`'s replay should measure against the *giver's* position carried in the instruction when the
   receiver has not yet reported its own — the same class the rejoin ticket records for carried items.

## The item is minted in the host's world, and it falls there before the instruction (read 2026-11-26 08:10)

Reading the receiver's half answers the third question above without a run. `RemoteIntentApplier.ApplyPickUpToSlot`
(`src/CasualtiesUnknownOnline.GameAdapter/RemoteIntentApplier.cs:246-287`) hands a world item to the game's own pickup —
`body.PickUpItem(item, targetSlot, false)` (`:279`) — so the guard that refuses is `Body.DoPickupCheck`, measured against
**the receiver's own body** and **the item's position in the receiver's world**. The item's position is not the giver's:
the host materialized it at `(21.5,395.5)`, ninety-six units below the placed spot `(7.96,491.42)`, because the host's
world had nothing under it and it fell. By the time the instruction is replayed the distance is real on both sides — the
receiver is not mis-measuring.

So the fix belongs where the item is minted, not in the replay's measuring: a hand-over that is meant for a slot either
travels as a slot destination (no world presence at all) or is held at the giver's own position until the receiver's
pickup takes it. Measuring against the giver's position in the replay would hide the fall rather than remove it, which is
why that option is recorded and not recommended.
