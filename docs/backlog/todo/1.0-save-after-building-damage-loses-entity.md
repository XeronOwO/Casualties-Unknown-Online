# Save after trap damage drops building health

- Status: Todo
- Priority: High
- Created: 2026-11-16

## Design

The restore contract is in `docs/design/cross-cutting/saves-and-recovery.md`:
the cut carries the host's world facts and Continue repairs them per entry.
The archive layout and the restore audit live in
`docs/architecture/save-archive-format.md`.

## Reading

The merged full suite (`.acceptance/mergedfull1/report/report.md`) placed
`save-continue` after `mine-blast`. The save itself passed behaviourally, but
the host logged two errors while restoring the continued world:

```
[SaveFacts] the live world did NOT take 2 of 3 restored world-entity row(s) —
the regenerated layer has no such entity where the cut recorded it, and those
facts are NOT in the live world.
```

The cut at `.acceptance/merged1/saves/w-20261115-bf62/live.cuoz` carries:

- `trap-consumption` at `(10,511)`;
- `building-health` at `(11,512)`, health `1895.8866`;
- `building-health` at `(-63,446)`, health `0`;
- `trap-state` at `(10,511)` kind `31`, phase `2`;
- `trap-state` at `(10,511)` kind `1`, phase `3`.

The live world took the trap consumption and refused both building-health
rows. The blocks, keypads, geysers, recipe unlocks, radiation line, and items
all restored.

## Why it matters

The restore audit is deliberately strict: a missing world-entity row means the
continued world is not the saved one. Today the full suite avoids the red by
running `save-continue` before `mine-blast` and the block mutations; that is a
test-order isolation, not a product fix. A player who saves after a trap
explosion can lose the damaged building's health and the next Continue reports
an incomplete restore.

## Acceptance

1. Reproduce with `save-continue` immediately after `mine-blast`; the restore
   must either apply both building-health rows or classify an already-destroyed
   building as intentionally absent without an error.
2. The restored live world must carry the same building health for every
   building the cut names, or the archive must omit rows that cannot exist in
   a regenerated layer.
3. The full suite keeps `save-continue` before the damage rows until the
   restore behaviour is fixed and covered by a dedicated case.
4. No new wire shape is required; this is the restore half of the existing
   world-entity facts, not a new message.
