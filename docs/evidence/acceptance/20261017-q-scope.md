## Method

member=True`). Each row was then driven one step at a time through the in-process driver — console verbs on
both clients, committed recipes for the gameplay states — and read back from the consoles' own answers, the
product's `/data get`, and the clients' logs. A member's answer that arrives a frame later was read in a
follow-up `console-read` call, never from the command call's own `lastLine` (the `/give` answers of rows 5,
