# The game's traps and events (1.0)

Cited from the 1.0 decompile (`reversing/release-1.0/Assembly-CSharp/`).

## Blocks take damage and show it

`BlockDamage` owns a block's damage and its sprite: the fraction `damage / block.health` selects one of the five
damage sprites (`BlockDamage.cs:8-41`), so a crack's look is a function of the block's own health table. A block's
health and the sprite set live on the world (`WorldGeneration.blockDamageSprites`), which is why a damage overlay and a
block-state table must agree on the same health.

## Crystals are rolled, not placed

1.0's world prefabs carry the crystal *look* but no `CrystalBehaviour`; the behaviour is what rolls the crystal's
properties:

- On generation completion the behaviour calls `SetUpEffects()` (`CrystalBehaviour.cs:81`), which replaces `effects`
  with a fresh list (`:102-104`).
- The roll can destroy the crystal outright: a 70% chance, or a crystal smaller than 0.4, or one placed in the central
  80%-height / 25%-width band of the map — any of these removes the component (`:105`).
- Otherwise it appends a weighted random effect (`GetRandomEffect`, `:85-90`) and writes the effects' names into the
  building's description (`:44-48`).
- Crystals are placed at generation; the behaviour then decides what each one *is*. A world generated by the game's own
  prefabs therefore shows crystals with no properties until the behaviour runs.

## Earthquakes

`WorldGeneration` owns the quake clock: a first delay of 240-1000 s (`:241`), then a delay of 600-1750 s scaled by the
run setting `timebetweenearthquakes` (`:990-1003`), skipped at depth 10 and beyond (`:991`); a quake lasts 3-25 s and
the intensity ramps toward 1 while it runs (`:1006-1011`). `allowEarthquakes` gates the whole thing (`:1004`).

## Traps

- A mine is a placed object with a pressed flag; the game explodes it natively and rolls its drops (read in the
  acceptance fixtures' paths: a mine's `pressed` set by an operator, the game's own explosion, a drop hold before the
  items settle).
- The bear trap (`BearTrap.cs`), barbed fences (`BarbedFence.cs`), sound/`GunmineScript` objects and the shrapnel
  minigame are the other trap-shaped objects; each owns its trigger and its damage.

## What the mod must respect

- Crystal properties are rolled at generation on the generating client; a mod that replicates a crystal must replicate
  the effect list, not the prefab.
- An explosion's drops are held by the trap for a short window before they settle; a save cut that runs inside that
  window is deferred by the game's own policy (see the cut seam).
- Earthquakes are a world-level event driven by the host's world clock; a member's copy follows the host's timing.

## Not covered here

- The full trap catalogue and each trap's numbers (damage, arming time, drop rolls): the files above carry them; this
  page maps the shapes.
- Sound cannons and their placement rules: named by the world's hazard rolls, not read in this pass.
